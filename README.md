
# My Favorite Movie Series

## The Hunger Games

**The Hunger Games** is one of my favorite movie series. It is about a world where young people are chosen to participate in a dangerous competition called the Hunger Games. The story follows Katniss Everdeen as she fights for survival and stands against an unfair government.

### Movies in the Series

1. The Hunger Games
   - **Catching Fire** ⭐ *(My Favorite)*
2. Mockingjay Part 1
3. Mockingjay Part 2
4. The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes

### About My Favorite Movie

#### The Hunger Games: Catching Fire

**Catching Fire** is my favorite movie in the series because of its exciting story and unexpected events. I like how Katniss and Peeta are forced to return to the arena for the 75th Hunger Games. The movie also shows how people begin to stand together against the Capitol.

### My Favorite Characters
- Katniss Everdeen
- Peeta Mellark
- Finnick Odair
- Johanna Mason

### Why I Like This Series

I like The Hunger Games because it has action, suspense, and meaningful lessons. It shows the importance of courage, survival, friendship, and standing up for what is right.
